Full Gas: Fart Runner

Full Gas Support Slide a finger horizontally to move. Keep it still for 0.5 seconds to charge; once charging, you can keep moving sideways. Lift your finger to attack. Watch your food choices, hunger and overfull state. Use Settings to change sound and game language. Local records are not cloud saves; do not uninstall the game as an initial troubleshooting step. Game Center is optional and does not block ordinary play when signed out. Report issues at https://github.com/myerwang/apps/issues . Include device model, system version, game version, screen and reproduction steps. Issues are public: do not attach passwords, private account details or personal save files.
