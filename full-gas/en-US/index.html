The chase is on, and holding it in is no longer an option! Run through a small town, slide your finger left and right, and choose what to eat. Balance hunger and an overfull belly. Keep your finger still for 0.5 seconds to charge, then lift it to attack your pursuers. Choose from three characters with different builds and talents. Twenty foods affect your fullness and abilities. Collect skill foods to develop and strengthen six attack styles. Every run is a new distance challenge. Your best distances and history are saved on this device. Sound and language settings are available in the game, with nine in-game languages. No ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Game Center is optional; ordinary play does not require Game Center login.
