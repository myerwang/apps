Privacy

Full Gas: Fart Runner

Full Gas Privacy On-device data The game stores best distances, run history, the last selected character, and sound and language settings on this device. Apple device-backup settings apply. Uninstalling the app may remove local data. Optional Game Center When you use Game Center, the game submits run distances through Apple services. Apple handles scores according to your Game Center identity and settings and may show your profile and ranking to other players. This does not make the game multiplayer. Ordinary play remains available without login or when the service is unavailable. Ads and payments This version has no ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions and uses no advertising tracking or developer-operated analytics service. Apple's App Store, Game Center and system diagnostics follow Apple's privacy policies and your system settings. Contact Send privacy or support questions to https://github.com/myerwang/apps/issues . Issues are public: do not include passwords, identity documents or private saves. GitHub account and website data follow GitHub's policies.
