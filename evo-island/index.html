Evo Island: Animal Journey

进化小岛

A single-player island adventure. Begin as a caterpillar, discover food, survive animal encounters and progress through 50 animal forms toward a T-Rex.

Explore grassland, woodland, swamp, canyon and volcano areas. Capture nests, recover health and replenish companions. Journeys and map seeds are saved on your device.

Available on iPhone and iPad after App Store approval. The first release is free, with no ads.
