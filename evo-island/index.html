Evo Planet: Animal Journey

Evo Planet: Animal Journey

Screenshots

Start as a tiny caterpillar and grow through an animal evolution journey toward mighty dinosaurs. Explore a spherical 3D world with meadows, forests, swamps, canyons and volcanic terrain. Search for food, face other creatures and claim nests as you grow. Take flight as a crow and see familiar landscapes from above. Choose unlocked animal forms and use the new previous-creature shortcut to switch between your two most recent forms. Plan your route with matching local and expanded maps. Continue your journey at the most recently visited nest that is still yours, with your earned progress preserved. Start a world from a seed or return to a saved journey. Touch controls, day and night, and different habitats accompany your single-player adventure.
