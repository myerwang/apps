Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Local game data · 本地游戏数据

The game stores journey progress, map seeds and audio settings on your device to continue your adventure. You can delete non-current journeys from History with confirmation. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to device backups managed by Apple.

旅程进度、地图种子和声音设置保存在设备中，用于继续游戏。可在历史旅程中二次确认删除非当前旅程。卸载会移除本地应用数据；Apple管理的设备备份另受其设置控制。

Game Center · Game Center

If you use Game Center, the game sends your highest level and achievement progress to Apple using your Game Center player identity. These data support leaderboards and achievements; your Game Center profile and scores may be visible to other players according to your Apple settings. This does not turn the game into multiplayer. Game Center is optional and an unavailable service does not block local play.

使用Game Center时，游戏通过您的Game Center玩家身份向Apple发送最高等级及成就进度，用于排行榜和成就。根据Apple账号设置，个人资料和成绩可能对其他玩家可见。Game Center为可选功能，不影响单人游戏。

See Apple’s Game Center privacy information. Manage Game Center access and your profile in device Settings.

Optional rewarded ads · 自愿激励广告

The original release contains no advertising. Versions with rewarded ads use Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Watching an ad is optional: it can waive experience loss from the current death or activate double attack power or double experience for 10–60 minutes (starting at 10 minutes, with longer rewards during the same session). Normal respawn remains available without an ad. Ads require internet access and may be unavailable.

原始首版没有广告。含激励广告的版本使用Google AdMob和Google用户消息平台（UMP）。观看广告为自愿选择，可免除本次死亡经验损失，或开启10至60分钟双倍攻击／双倍经验（首次10分钟，同次游戏中后续奖励逐步延长）；不看广告仍可正常复活。广告需联网，且可能暂不可用。

Google’s advertising SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic or performance information for advertising, measurement and service operation. Applicable consent choices are presented through UMP. Required privacy options can be reopened from the game’s privacy entry. Ad-enabled versions request Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission when applicable before advertising partners use activity across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising measurement. Tracking requires your ATT permission and all other applicable consent. Declining does not prevent normal play or free respawn. Any available ads must respect your choices. You can change tracking permission in device Settings.

Google广告SDK可能处理IP推算的大致位置、设备标识、广告交互及诊断或性能信息，用于广告、衡量及服务运行。适用的意见征求由UMP展示，可通过游戏隐私入口重新打开所需选项。含广告的版本会在适用时通过Apple应用跟踪透明度（ATT）请求许可，再允许广告合作伙伴使用您在其他公司的App和网站中的活动衡量广告效果。跟踪必须获得您的ATT许可并满足其他适用意见征求条件。拒绝不影响普通游戏或免费复活；可用广告必须遵守您的选择。您可以在设备系统设置中修改跟踪许可。

Read Google’s privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. Local journey data and optional Game Center remain separate from ad rewards; no separate player-account server is operated.

本地旅程和可选Game Center与广告奖励分开处理，没有独立玩家账号服务器。游戏玩法不请求位置、通讯录、摄像头、麦克风或相册权限。
