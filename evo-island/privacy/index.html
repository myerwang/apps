Privacy

Evo Planet: Animal Journey

Privacy Policy — Evo Planet Local data Journey progress, map seeds and audio settings are stored on your device. You can delete a non-current journey from History after confirmation. Uninstalling removes local app data, subject to backups managed by Apple. Game Center If enabled, your highest level and achievement progress are sent to Apple with your Game Center player identity for leaderboards and achievements. Your profile and scores may be visible according to your Apple settings. Game Center is optional; its unavailability does not block local single-player play. Manage it in device Settings. Optional rewarded ads Version 1.2.0 uses Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP). Watching an ad is optional: waive experience loss for the current death, or receive double attack or double experience for 10–60 minutes. The first boost lasts 10 minutes; subsequent rewards in the same session can be longer. Timers continue while the app is closed or in the background. Normal respawn remains available without ads. Ads require internet and may be unavailable. Google's advertising SDK may process approximate location inferred from IP, device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information for advertising, measurement and service operation. UMP presents applicable consent choices. Required privacy options can be reopened from the game's privacy entry. Where applicable, Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission is requested before partners use activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising measurement. Tracking requires ATT permission and other applicable consent. Refusal does not block normal play or free respawn; available ads must respect your choices. Tracking permission can be changed in device Settings. Local journeys and optional Game Center are separate from ad rewards. No separate player-account server is operated. Gameplay does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo-library permissions. Apple services and support Distribution, system services and diagnostics you choose to share with Apple follow Apple's policies and device settings. If you email support, we use only the information you voluntarily provide to answer and investigate your request. Send only what is necessary; do not send passwords or sensitive information.
