Journeys and controls · 旅程与操作

Use the left joystick to move, drag the right side to look, and use the Attack and Jump buttons. Use Continue to resume the current journey. History lets you revisit saved journeys and delete non-current journeys after confirmation.

左侧摇杆移动，拖动右侧调整视角，使用攻击与跳跃按钮。继续可恢复当前旅程；历史旅程支持查看和二次确认删除非当前旅程。

Game Center

Game Center is optional. Offline play and cancelled sign-in do not prevent the single-player game. Check your Game Center account in system Settings if sign-in is unavailable.

Game Center为可选功能；离线或取消登录仍可进行单人游戏。登录异常时可检查系统设置中的Game Center账号。
